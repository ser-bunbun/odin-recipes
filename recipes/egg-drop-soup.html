Egg Drop Soup

A simple, silky soup ready in about 15 minutes.

Serves: 2 to 3

Ingredients

  • 4 cups chicken or vegetable broth
  • 2 eggs, lightly beaten
  • 1 tbsp butter
  • 2 tbsp cornstarch mixed with 3 tbsp water
  • 1/2 tsp white pepper
  • 1/2 tsp sesame oil
  • 1 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tsp soy sauce
  • 1 green onion, sliced

Instructions

  1. Bring broth to a gentle boil in a pot.
  2. Stir in soy sauce and white pepper.
  3. Slowly stream in the cornstarch slurry while stirring, until soup thickens slightly.
  4. Reduce heat to low. Slowly drizzle in the beaten egg in a thin stream while gently stirring in one direction to form ribbons.
  5. Remove from heat and stir in sesame oil. Season with salt to taste.
  6. Garnish with green onion before serving.

Notes

  • Pour the egg slowly and stir gently — rushing this step makes clumps instead of ribbons.
  • For extra richness, stir in a small pat of butter at the end.
