Creamy Miso Udon

A quick, comforting noodle dish — ready in about 15 minutes.

Serves: 2

Ingredients

  • 2 packs fresh or frozen udon noodles
  • 2 tbsp white miso paste
  • 1 tbsp butter
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1/2 cup heavy cream (or oat milk for lighter version)
  • 1/4 cup reserved noodle water
  • 1 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 soft-boiled egg (optional, per serving)
  • 1 green onion, sliced
  • Sesame seeds, for garnish
  • Chili oil, optional

Instructions

  1. Boil udon noodles according to package instructions. Reserve 1/4 cup of the noodle water before draining.
  2. In a pan over medium heat, melt butter and sauté garlic until fragrant, about 1 minute.
  3. Lower heat and whisk in miso paste until smooth.
  4. Add heavy cream, soy sauce, and reserved noodle water. Stir until the sauce is smooth and creamy.
  5. Add drained noodles to the pan and toss until fully coated.
  6. Plate and top with a soft-boiled egg, sliced green onion, sesame seeds, and a drizzle of chili oil if desired.

Notes

  • Add sautéed mushrooms or spinach for extra texture.
  • Swap heavy cream for coconut milk for a dairy-free version.
