Spicy Sichuan Cucumbers

A crunchy, cold appetizer with a numbing kick.

Serves: 2

Ingredients

  • 2 Cucumbers
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 1 tbsp butter
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp black vinegar
  • 1 tsp sugar
  • 1 tsp sesame oil
  • 1 tbsp chili oil
  • Sesame seeds, for garnish
  • Cilanro, chopped for garnish

Instructions

  1. Smash cucumbers with the flat side of a knife until they split, then cut into bite-sized chunks.
  2. Toss cucumbers with salt and let sit for 10 minutes to draw out excess water. Drain any liquid.
  3. In a small bowl, mix garlic, soy sauce, black vinegar, sugar, sesame oil, chili oil, and crushed Sichuan peppercorns.
  4. Pour the dressing over the cucumbers and toss well to coat.
  5. Let marinate for at least 10 minutes before serving.
  6. Garnish with cilantro and sesame seeds.

Notes

  • Toast Sichuan peppercorns in a dry pan for 1 to 2 minutes until fragrant before crushing for the best flavor.
  • Chill before serving for extra crispness.
